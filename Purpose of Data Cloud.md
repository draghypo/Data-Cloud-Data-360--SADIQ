                                    Purpose of Salesforce Data Cloud/Data 360
                                    -----------------------------------------


Core Purpose: - Salesforce Data Cloud is a centralized data platform designed to pull and connect customer information                     from multiple disparate sources into one single place.
      It creates a Unified Customer Profile so that human users (Service & Sales Agents) and AI Agents have a                       complete 360-degree view of the customer without jumping between systems.

Why It Came to Market: - 
    *Eliminate Data Silos: Customer data traditionally lives scattered across multiple platforms (e.g., Amazon S3, Snowflake,                            Databricks, external ERPs, and CRMs).

    *Single Source of Truth: Allows teams to view all interactions, transactions, and behavior in one unified place.

Simple word: - Instead of searching through 10 different notebooks scattered across different rooms for a single customer, Data Cloud automatically collects all those pages and stitches them into one master folder so anyone opening it sees everything instantly.
